# SLAS Study Notebook

Offline study app for *Statistical Learning for Automation Systems*: theory by lecture, flashcards, quiz, past exams with solutions, blackboard sheets.

Open the site once while online, then add it to the home screen. After that it works without internet.

## Slides

The Slides tab has study notes for every slide of Lectures 0–5 (`slides.js`). The slide images are copyrighted by the lecturer and are **not** in this public repository; they are shown only in the private version of the app.
